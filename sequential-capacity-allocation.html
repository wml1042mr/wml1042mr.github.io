Research

Sequential Capacity Allocation under Arrival Uncertainty

  1. Predict
  2. Optimize
  3. Observe
  4. Update

A data-driven framework for sequential admission decisions under uncertain applicant behavior. The project integrates enrollment prediction with stochastic dynamic optimization, allowing admission decisions to adapt as new applicants arrive and deposit information becomes available.

Problem

Admission decisions are made sequentially before final enrollment outcomes are known. The decision maker must balance enrollment targets, cohort quality, and the risks created by uncertain applicant yield.

Approach

Machine-learning models estimate applicant enrollment behavior, while a dynamic optimization framework determines when to admit, wait, or defer decisions as information evolves over time.

Methods

  • Machine Learning
  • Stochastic Dynamic Programming
  • Approximate Dynamic Programming
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Sequential Decision Making
