A professional introduction

About.

I am a PhD candidate in Operations & Decision Technologies at the Paul Merage School of Business, University of California, Irvine. My work combines optimization, machine learning, and data-driven decision making to address complex operational problems under uncertainty.

My research focuses on sequential decision making, resource allocation, forecasting, and optimization, with applications spanning higher education, supply chain operations, and healthcare.

Before UC Irvine, I earned an M.S. in Supply Chain Management from Washington University in St. Louis. My prior work spans healthcare operations, fresh-food e-commerce logistics, credit-risk modeling, and consulting projects in digital growth, process automation, and nonprofit operations.
