Research

Agricultural Supply Chain Optimization

  1. Forecast
  2. Schedule
  3. Balance Capacity

A forecasting and optimization framework for agricultural production scheduling under uncertain growing conditions and storage-capacity constraints. The project combines time-series forecasting with mixed-integer optimization to coordinate planting and harvest decisions across a large portfolio of seed populations.

Problem

Harvest timing varies with weather and growing conditions, creating mismatch between weekly production volume and available storage capacity. The planning problem requires coordinating many seed populations while accounting for operational capacity constraints.

Approach

Temperature forecasts generated from historical weather data are incorporated into a mixed-integer optimization model that schedules agricultural production to reduce capacity mismatch and improve utilization across the planning horizon.
